## This is my first ever **blog**. 

In an age where everyone is increasingly using AI to write everything for them, I think it is getting more and more important to write down our own thoughts without any help from AI.

> But why should we even write things if AI can do it exceptionally well?

Well, for starters, ai writing sucks. 

Well it doesn't actually suck but imagine everyone suddenly starts using `delve` in their vocabulary.

and everyone starts sounding the same.

What would separate you from the others then?

>  But we can still prompt AI  to sound a bit different; tell it to sound human, etc. etc.

Yes, that will help to an extent. But are we becoming more human or more robotic in the process?


![[human2ai.svg|410|invert]]


I am a belieber in the capabilities and power of ai. It has changed the way we all work and it has made so many things easier for us. We no longer have to spend a lot of time on manual grunt work and can  focus more on the fun stuff like thinking about creative ideas and moving at a much faster pace with our projects.

**But writing is not one of them**.  When we write, we collect all our thoughts and we use our human brains to put that information in an understandable and interpretable way and in this process we become more intelligent.

We learn to make sense of all the information in our head and organize it in a truly effective way. It doesn't even have to be something technical. It can be about anything that we like. What matters is that we practice the art of putting our thoughts in an organized way in front of the world (if it's in front of people we won't be sloppy with it)

And well I think it one of the most important defense mechanisms against `brain rot`.

And who knows, someone might read your blog and get a nice creative idea or you yourself might get one in the process.

 Remember, writing is one of the most creative and human processes that exists. it is one of the ways we can blurt out our thoughts and ideas and feelings and imagination and anything else that we can't easily express through other forms of media.

> Don't offload a part of **you** to AI

But yeah, enough of a tangent, i just wanted something to write so i could publish my first blog :p
